Every post has a ready-to-post caption and one format: a Canva graphic, a video or a Reel. Tap Copy caption on any post, then paste it into your own post.

How to save a graphic, video or Reel

Android: Tap the Save image button under the graphic. It saves to the Download album in your Gallery or Photos app.

iPhone: Tap the Save image button under the graphic, then tap Save Image. It goes to your Photos.

Windows computer: Click the Save image button under the graphic. It saves to your Downloads folder.

Mac: Click the Save image button under the graphic. It saves to your Downloads folder. To put it in Photos instead, Control-click the graphic in Safari and choose Add Image to Photos.

Videos and Reels: Tap the Save video button under the video or Reel (on iPhone, then tap Save Video). It saves the same places as graphics. On a computer, the video may open first: right-click it (on a Mac, Control-click) and choose Save video as, or use the ⋮ menu and choose Download. Add it to your post and paste in the caption with Copy caption.
